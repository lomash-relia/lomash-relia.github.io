## Autonomous Systems & Robotics Researcher

I am an M.Tech student in **Autonomous Systems and Machine Intelligence** at

My work focuses on **robotic perception, control systems, and edge AI**, with hands-on experience in **simulation-to-real deployment**, particularly for **space and planetary robotics**.
