I am an M.Tech student in **Autonomous Systems and Machine Intelligence** at  
**Atal Bihari Vajpayee Indian Institute of Information Technology and Management (ABV-IIITM), Gwalior**, with a strong focus on **robotics R&D** at the intersection of **perception, control, and real-world deployment**.

My work centers on **building end-to-end autonomous robotic systems**, where I integrate **computer vision, control theory, and embedded AI** to move from **simulation and mathematical modeling** to **hardware-constrained, real-world execution**. I am particularly interested in problems where perception must directly inform control decisions under uncertainty.

---

### Technical Focus
- Robotic manipulation and control using **MuJoCo**
- Vision-based control and **Image-Based Visual Servoing (IBVS)**
- **Edge AI** deployment on embedded platforms
- Autonomous navigation and perception for **space and planetary robotics**

---

### Research & Industry Experience

I worked as a **Research Intern at the Space Applications Centre (ISRO), Ahmedabad**, where I contributed to the development of a **prototype planetary rover**. My work involved integrating **monocular depth estimation and object detection** with onboard computation, and optimizing deep learning models for **near real-time inference under thermal and resource constraints**. This experience strengthened my understanding of **system-level trade-offs** between perception accuracy, inference latency, and hardware limitations.

I have also collaborated with the **Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology (IITM), Pune**, on deep learning–based **tropical cyclone intensity estimation** using multi-channel satellite imagery. Through this work, I gained experience in **data-driven modeling, performance benchmarking, and reproducible experimentation** in applied computer vision research.

---

### Current Work

As part of my **Master’s Thesis**, I am developing **mathematical control systems for robotic manipulation** using the SO-101 robot arm in MuJoCo, with a focus on **vision-based feedback control** and system stability. In parallel, I am exploring **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models** for potential applications in **space and planetary robotics**.

Alongside research, I serve as a **Teaching Assistant**, supporting undergraduate laboratory sessions and tutorials, which has strengthened my ability to explain complex technical concepts clearly and systematically.

### Research Interests & Career Direction

My broader interests include **space robotics, embodied AI, physical AI systems, and simulation-to-real transfer**. I am particularly motivated by problems that require **tight integration between perception and control** in challenging, resource-constrained environments.

I am actively seeking **Robotics R&D or Research Engineer roles** where I can contribute to the design, analysis, and deployment of **reliable autonomous systems** that operate in real-world conditions.
