I am an M.Tech student in **Autonomous Systems and Machine Intelligence** at **Atal Bihari Vajpayee Indian Institute of Information Technology and Management (ABV-IIITM), Gwalior**, with a strong interest in **robotics research and development**, particularly in **autonomous systems, robotic perception, control, and edge AI**.

My academic and research work focuses on building **end-to-end robotic systems**, covering the complete pipeline from **simulation and mathematical modeling** to **real-world deployment on embedded hardware**. I have hands-on experience with **robotic manipulation, vision-based control, and autonomous navigation**, using tools such as **MuJoCo, ROS2, OpenCV, PyTorch, and Raspberry Pi**.

I have worked as a **Research Intern at the Space Applications Centre (ISRO), Ahmedabad**, where I developed a **prototype planetary rover** with monocular depth estimation and onboard edge AI. My work involved optimizing deep learning models for **real-time inference under thermal and resource constraints**, and designing modular control architectures for both autonomous and manual operation.

I have also collaborated with the **Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology (IITM), Pune**, where I developed deep learning pipelines for **tropical cyclone intensity estimation** using multi-channel satellite imagery. This work strengthened my understanding of **computer vision, data-driven modeling, and performance benchmarking**.

Currently, as part of my **Master’s Thesis Project**, I am developing **mathematical control systems for robotic manipulation** using the SO-101 robot arm in MuJoCo, and exploring **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models** for applications in **space and planetary robotics**. Alongside research, I also serve as a **Teaching Assistant**, supporting undergraduate laboratories and tutorials.

My broader interests include **space robotics, embodied AI, physical AI systems, and simulation-to-real transfer**. I am actively seeking **Robotics R&D or Research Engineer roles** where I can contribute to the design and deployment of reliable autonomous systems in real-world environments.

For more details, please refer to my **Projects**, **Research**, and **CV** sections.
