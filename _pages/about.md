## How I Build Autonomous Systems

I approach robotics as a systems engineering problem - not just a machine learning problem.

For a robotic system to operate reliably, perception, control, computation, and hardware constraints must be designed together. I focus on building pipelines that remain stable and predictable when exposed to real-world latency, power limits, and sensor uncertainty.

---

### My Engineering Approach

**1. Start from the Control Objective**  
Define what stability, convergence, or performance actually means in measurable terms.

**2. Validate in Simulation**  
Model kinematics, camera geometry, and control laws under controlled conditions.

**3. Deploy Under Constraints**  
Move to hardware and test under realistic power, thermal, and computation limits.

**4. Measure Everything**  
Latency, convergence time, failure modes, and repeatability matter more than isolated demos.

---

### What I Value

- Mathematical grounding behind algorithms  
- Clear system architecture  
- Stability over novelty  
- Deployment over demonstration  

---

I am particularly motivated by problems in space robotics, physical AI, and perception-driven control systems where reliability is non-negotiable.
