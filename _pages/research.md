## Depth-Aware Rover: Edge AI and Monocular Vision for Real-World Implementation  
*Springer | IEEE INGARSS 2025*

This research presents a **depth-aware autonomous rover system** designed under real-world hardware and thermal constraints.

**Summary:**
- Developed a low-cost rover platform using **monocular vision** and **edge AI** on Raspberry Pi  
- Compared **stereo-based depth perception in simulation** with **monocular depth estimation in real-world deployment**  
- Integrated object detection and depth estimation for navigation under constrained compute and power budgets  

**Key Contributions:**
- Demonstrated **system-level trade-offs** between depth accuracy, inference latency, and thermal stability  
- Highlighted challenges in **simulation-to-real transfer** for depth-based navigation  
- Provided insights into **edge deployment feasibility** for autonomous exploration platforms  

**Publications & Presentations:**
- Springer Book Chapter (July 2024)  
- IEEE INGARSS 2025 Symposium (Oral / Poster)  

🔗 Springer: https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-96-3420-0_17

## Deep Learning Models for Tropical Cyclone Intensity Estimation  
*WGNE Blue Book | IITM Collaboration*

This work focuses on applying deep learning techniques to **multi-channel satellite imagery** for estimating tropical cyclone intensity.

**Summary:**
- Designed a **4-channel image-to-intensity regression pipeline** using deep convolutional networks  
- Evaluated rotation-blended CNN architectures from existing literature  
- Benchmarked experimental results against published performance metrics  

**Key Contributions:**
- Systematic replication and validation of prior deep learning approaches  
- Analysis of model robustness on complex meteorological data  
- Emphasis on reproducibility and scientific benchmarking  

**Publication:**
- WGNE Blue Book (November 2024)

🔗 WGNE Paper:  
https://wgne.net/bluebook/uploads/2024/docs/010_Relia,_Lomash_Evaluating_Deep_Learning_Models_for_Cyclone_Intensity_Estimation.pdf

## Master’s Thesis Project (Ongoing)  
*ABV-IIITM Gwalior*

As part of my M.Tech program, I am currently working on a thesis project focused on **robotic manipulation and autonomous control**.

**Research Focus:**
- Mathematical control systems for robotic manipulators using **MuJoCo-based simulation**  
- **Eye-in-hand visual servoing** and perception-driven control strategies  
- Exploration of **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models** for robotic manipulation in **space and planetary environments**  

**Objectives:**
- Develop robust control pipelines that integrate perception and action  
- Study the applicability of foundation models to embodied robotic systems  
- Improve reliability and generalization in simulated and real-world manipulation tasks
